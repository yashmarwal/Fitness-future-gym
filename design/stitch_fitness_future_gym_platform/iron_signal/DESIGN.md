## Brand & Style

This design system delivers an unvarnished, heavy-duty digital experience built for raw, high-intensity strength culture. Tailored for an authentic, unisex powerlifting and athletic training facility, the aesthetic is rooted in industrial grit, uncompromising utility, and physical discipline. It strips away the soft, hyper-polished SaaS tropes—discarding rounded pills, decorative pastel glows, and sterile corporate minimalism—in favor of structural authority and athletic power.

The visual direction draws from high-contrast functional brutalism and industrial signage:
- **Tone:** Aggressive, uncompromising, kinetic, and authentic.
- **Audience:** Serious lifters, conditioning athletes, and individuals committed to progressive overload and functional power.
- **Emotional Impact:** The interface commands urgency, focus, and physical readiness through razor-sharp geometry, towering condensed typography, and a single molten signal-orange accent against deep cast-iron tones.

## Colors

The palette is constructed around deep kiln and cast-iron foundations punctuated by a singular, high-visibility warning orange. Pure digital black (`#000000`) is explicitly avoided to preserve material warmth and tactile depth.

### Surface Architecture
- **Base Background (`#0B0A08`):** Deep warm near-black, mimicking raw rubber gym flooring and darkened iron plates.
- **Surface Muted (`#151310`):** Secondary backing for nested panels, table headers, and structural wells.
- **Surface / Card (`#1C1A16`):** Elevated interactive modules, content containers, and stat blocks.
- **Border / Divider Base (`#2B2822`):** Structural boundary lines providing crisp, low-glare definition.

### Signal & Accent Mechanics
- **Dominant Accent (`#FF5A1F`):** Molten signal-orange. High-voltage energetic color reserved strictly for critical conversions, kinetic metrics, active states, and focal rule lines.
- **Accent Interactive (`#B8410F`):** Pressed and focused state variant, delivering visual weight under pressure.
- **Primary Text (`#F5F1E8`):** Warm bone white, delivering sharp, high-contrast legibility across dark surfaces without eye fatigue.
- **Muted Text (`#8A8577`):** Industrial chalk-gray for secondary specs, unit markers, timestamps, and structural metadata.

### Application Rules
Never apply decorative multi-hue gradients or neon diffusion. Color must function strictly as physical feedback or high-priority signaling. Thin 1px `#FF5A1F` lines may be used as architectural anchor strokes on active boundaries or heroic callouts.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy establishes clear contrast between high-impact kinetic condensed headers and hyper-readable, neutral body text.

- **Display & Headlines (Bebas Neue):** Rendered strictly uppercase with tight, commanding vertical cadences. Designed to mirror strength record boards, plate weights, and physical signage.
- **Labels & Micro-Data (Oswald):** Used for tags, data tables, metrics, tracking indicators, and button text. Always set in uppercase with deliberate tracking (`0.06em` to `0.12em`) for immediate readability during quick visual sweeps.
- **Body & Editorial (Inter):** Sentence case, neutral, and unembellished. Prioritizes rapid legibility for membership tiers, training programs, and coaching dossiers.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a rigid 12-column fluid grid system that prioritizes dense information architectures, robust alignment lines, and assertive visual breaks.

### Screen Adaptations & Breakpoints
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with `1rem` edge margins and `1rem` gutters. Content cascades linearly with stacked card structures and edge-to-edge section dividers.
- **Tablet (768px – 1024px):** 8-column layout with `1.5rem` edge margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Metric clusters reflow into 2x2 grids.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12-column layout maxing out at `80rem` (1280px) centered width with `1.5rem` gutters. Modules snap cleanly along vertical column borders.

### Spatial Discipline
Layout elements adhere strictly to a dense 4px/8px baseline grid. Content blocks must feel grounded and structural; avoid arbitrary padding that generates empty, floaty space. Sections are framed by crisp `1px` borders (`#2B2822`) rather than loose air gaps.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids decorative blur effects, diffused dropped shadows, and soft neomorphic indents. Depth is constructed purely through **tonal layering**, **structural surface steps**, and **high-contrast mechanical boundaries**.

### Surface Stacking Model
- **Ground Floor (`#0B0A08`):** Canvas backdrop across all primary viewports.
- **Layer 1 - Inset Wells (`#151310`):** Recessed surfaces for search bars, data tables, schedule tracks, and secondary modules.
- **Layer 2 - Structural Cards (`#1C1A16`):** Standard interactive panels and hero sections, outlined with a 1px border of `#2B2822`.
- **Layer 3 - Floating Modals & Overlays (`#1C1A16`):** Solid surfaces with an intensified 1px stroke of `#FF5A1F` or `#3E3A32` accompanied by a hard, 0-blur drop-offset shadow: `4px 4px 0px #000000`.

### The Molten Accent Rule
Active selections, key performance banners, and pinned containers utilize an anchor accent: a crisp 1px `#FF5A1F` top or left border that serves as a visual signal of focus.

## Shapes

Every element across the design system employs **sharp, angular geometry (0px border radius)** or a maximum strict restraint of 2px for minor micro-components. 

- Pill-shaped buttons, rounded badge capsules, and soft card corners are forbidden.
- Shapes reflect the hard angles of barbells, steel racks, and weight plates.
- Decorative indicators utilize geometric cut-corners (45-degree chamfers) or square tick marks rather than circular dots.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid `#FF5A1F` background, `#0B0A08` bold uppercase text (`Oswald`), 0px border-radius, `0.75rem 1.5rem` padding. Hover: `#B8410F` background with slight leftward translate or hard black 2px drop-offset shadow.
- **Secondary Action:** `#1C1A16` background, 1px solid `#2B2822` border, `#F5F1E8` uppercase text. Hover: border shifts to `#FF5A1F`, text shifts to `#FF5A1F`.
- **Ghost / Utility:** Transparent background, no border, `#8A8577` text. Hover: `#F5F1E8` with an active 2px bottom border rule in `#FF5A1F`.

### Input Fields
- Dark well background (`#151310`), 1px solid `#2B2822` border, 0px border radius.
- Text: `#F5F1E8` in `Inter`, labels: uppercase `#8A8577` in `Oswald`.
- Focus State: 1px continuous outline of `#FF5A1F`; zero blurry focus rings.

### Cards & Modules
- Backing: `#1C1A16` with a uniform 1px `#2B2822` border.
- Optional Hero Framing: Top border stroke reinforced with a 2px `#FF5A1F` accent bar.
- Padding: Dense `1.25rem` to `1.5rem`.

### Chips & Badges
- Background: `#151310` with a 1px solid `#2B2822` border.
- Text: `label-sm` uppercase in `#8A8577`.
- Selected/Active State: Solid `#FF5A1F` with dark `#0B0A08` text, or 1px `#FF5A1F` border with `#FF5A1F` text. Strictly squared edges.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Box/Frame: Square (0px radius) 16x16px boundary, 1px solid `#2B2822`, background `#151310`.
- Checked State: Solid `#FF5A1F` fill with a stark black checkmark, or solid inner square fill for radios. Never use circular geometries.

### Gym-Specific Custom Components
- **PR / Metric Display Block:** Heavy `#1C1A16` tile framing a massive stat figure in `Bebas Neue` (`#FF5A1F`), paired with a secondary chalk-gray `#8A8577` unit marker (e.g., `KG`, `REPS`) and a 1px `#2B2822` bottom baseline separator.
- **Slot / Class Roster Table:** Row-based list with `#151310` alternating stripe contrast, separated by 1px `#2B2822` horizontal dividers, featuring a real-time availability indicator formatted as a solid orange square (available) or muted charcoal square (full).
